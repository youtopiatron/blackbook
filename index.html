Bad evidence uses small 7 × 0.85, medium 18 × 0.75, and severe 40 × 0.60 successive same-size values; remediated severe incidents contribute 25%. Good evidence is 4/11/22 points, half without a second independent source, and one quarter for placation. Totals cap at 100 bad and 44 good; offsets at 20% of bad. The evidence ceiling is max(1, 100 − 1.25 × net bad). Non-Credible standing caps Regard at 66.

Standing is Unverified until there are at least two independent sources and 11 total gross evidence points; then Concerning starts at net bad 36, Mixed at 11–below 36, and Credible requires net bad below 11, 22 unplacated good points, two sources, no unremediated severe incident, and confirming evidence within three years.

    $60M annual investment in Black communities; reparations fund created Jan 2026 (though unfunded due to $1B deficit). Dream Keeper initiative restored.
  • Documented hate group presence (county-level) — legacy score 2/5
    Two hate groups based in SF; seven in wider Bay Area. Lower concentration than Southern CA counties but present.
